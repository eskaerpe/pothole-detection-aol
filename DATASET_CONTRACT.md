# Dataset contract pointer

The complete handoff/training/evaluation requirements are in [docs/ai-and-dataset.md](docs/ai-and-dataset.md). Dataset validation and real model pilot are tracked in [phase 08](plan/phases/08-dataset-and-model-pilot.md).

Dataset contributor supplies versioned, traceable labeled data and independent videos; implementer validates compatibility and reports concrete failures. Preserve raw originals, grouped splits and licensing evidence. Neither dataset collection nor this pointer authorizes extraction, training or GPU use.
